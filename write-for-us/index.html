Contribute to Insights

Submission guidelines for lawyers, academics, and legal professionals

Are you a member of the legal fraternity with an article, paper, or commentary to share? We welcome submissions from lawyers, academics, and legal professionals for consideration on our Insights page.

Submission Guidelines

  • We look for original articles and commentary on Pakistani and international legal developments — corporate law, taxation, dispute resolution, technology and data protection, or any other area of practice.
  • Please email your submission as a Word file attachment to articles@farzillaw.com.
  • Articles should ideally be 800–1,500 words, exploring one topic or idea.
  • We only accept fresh, original work that has not been published or submitted elsewhere, in print or online.
  • Editorial Policy on AI: We allow up to 19% AI-generated content in your submission — AI may assist with streamlining and proofreading, provided your creative and authentic insights remain the core of the piece.
  • Please include a short bio — your qualifications, experience, and any relevant achievements — along with a square headshot photo (plain background) as a separate attachment.
  • Due to the volume of submissions we receive, we may not be able to respond to every email individually.
