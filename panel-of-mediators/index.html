Panel of Mediators

Accredited professionals affiliated with FARZIL LAW | Centre for Dispute Resolution - FLCDR

Ali Mohiuddin

Distinguished Member, Panel of Mediators

Accredited Civil/Commercial Mediator and Associate Arbitrator trained through ADR ODR International, holding triple international accreditation from SIMI, IMI, and CMC UK. He is also a Gazetted Mediator accredited by the Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of Pakistan.

He serves as Secretary of the Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) Committee for the District Bar Association Rawalpindi, where he leads institutional development, coordinates capacity-building programmes, and promotes mediation and arbitration to enhance access to justice. He is an Advocate and Associate Partner at Mohiuddin & Mohiuddin Advocates & Litigators, holding an LL.B. (Hons) from the University of Bedfordshire, United Kingdom.

He also serves as a Member of the Chairman's Initiative Team within the Human Rights Committee of the Punjab Bar Council, providing expert legal research, policy support, and advisory services.

Member: Young ICCA, Young ICSID, Young WIPO ADR, and the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (CIArb).

Fahad Khan Sherwani

Member, Panel of Mediators

Accredited Civil/Commercial Mediator and Associate Arbitrator trained through ADR ODR International, with triple international accreditation through SIMI, IMI and CMC UK. He is also a Gazette-notified Accredited Mediator under the Alternative Dispute Resolution (Accreditation) Rules, 2023, accredited by the Ministry of Law & Justice, Government of Pakistan.

As an Advocate specialising in international business law, commercial law and dispute resolution, Fahad combines practical legal experience with specialised training in mediation, arbitration and contemporary ADR mechanisms. His areas of ADR interest and practice include commercial and corporate disputes, contractual disputes, civil and business disputes, international and cross-border commercial disputes, banking and financial services disputes, corporate governance, intellectual property and technology-related disputes, and employment matters. He holds an LL.M. in International Business Law from the University of Bedfordshire, United Kingdom, with academic focus including international commercial arbitration, international trade law, international commercial litigation, and intellectual property law, along with an LL.B. (Hons) from the University of Bedfordshire.

Fahad serves as a Member of the Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) Committee of the District Bar Association Rawalpindi, contributing to initiatives promoting mediation, arbitration, amicable settlement, and access to efficient dispute resolution. He also serves as a Member of the Chairman’s Initiative Team, Human Rights Committee, Punjab Bar Council, providing legal research, policy and advisory support.

His professional development includes specialised training in international commercial arbitration, mediation, ODR and emerging global practices in dispute resolution, including programmes and workshops associated with WIPO, UNCITRAL, the U.S. Department of Commerce CLDP, ITCILO, Saarosh ADR Centre and ADR ODR International.

Member: Young ICCA, Young ICSID, WIPO ADR Young, the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (CIArb), and ADR ODR International.

Shah Mir Ali

Member, Panel of Mediators

Triple-Accredited Mediator and Associate Arbitrator, and Legal Attorney at Unitzero Pvt Ltd. He serves as Karachi Chapter Head of the AOI × ICDRL Peacemakers Community (AIPC).

His mediation training includes the Mediation and Negotiation Program Pakistan, triple-accredited Civil/Commercial Mediation Training by ADR ODR International & ICDRL, 24-Hour Up-Level Mediation Training by Kathleen Ruane Leedy, and Global Mediation Training. In arbitration, he has completed International Commercial Arbitration Training by ADR ODR International & ICDRL and the International Commercial Arbitration Program by MCB Sri Lanka.

He serves as Trainer and Expert Assessor at the Mediation and Negotiation Program 2026 and at the Karakoram Institute of Dispute Resolution Mediation & Negotiation Competition 2026, as Expert Assessor at the Teen Mediation International Mediation & Negotiation Competition, and as Trainer at the 3-Day Arbitration and Mediation Training organised by Legal Reformers and Denco Global Institute.

As a coach he has worked with APITT Law School (Sri Lanka) for the ADC–ICC Asia-Pacific Commercial Mediation Competition (APCMC) 2025, and with Digital Peacemakers, organised by Teen Mediation International. He has sat as Arbitrator at the London Pre-Moot 2026, the 2nd VIS Moot Pre-Moot, the Asia-Pacific Vis Pre-Moot 2026, and the Maldives Moot Court Society (MMCS) International Maritime Arbitration Competition 2025.

Shah Mir Ali combines formal ADR training with practical experience in mediation education, coaching, advocacy, and international arbitration, with a particular interest in developing mediation and dispute-resolution practice among emerging legal professionals.
