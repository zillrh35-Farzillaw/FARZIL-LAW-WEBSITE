Farrukh Mushtaq

Attorney-In-Charge

Advocate High Courts of Pakistan | Corporate & Technology Lawyer — Litigation • Corporate • Consumer • Procurement • Civil • Medical Negligence • Criminal & Commercial Disputes

I am a litigation and dispute-resolution lawyer practicing in Pakistan with experience in civil, criminal, corporate, consumer, procurement, and commercial matters. My practice primarily focuses on strategic litigation, legal advisory, dispute management, and courtroom advocacy.

Over time, I have worked on a wide range of matters involving consumer protection, procurement disputes, criminal litigation, property and contractual disputes, corporate advisory, and legal compliance. I believe effective legal representation requires more than knowledge of statutes and precedents — it requires strategy, preparation, commercial understanding, and practical problem-solving tailored to each client’s circumstances.

In addition to litigation, I also take interest in mediation and arbitration as effective mechanisms for resolving disputes efficiently while preserving professional and business relationships. Through my professional engagement and legal practice, I aim to contribute toward greater legal awareness, responsible advocacy, and practical legal solutions.
